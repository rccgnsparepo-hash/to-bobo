# Flick Audio Notification Assets

Place your notification and launch audio clips in this directory:

1. `welcome-to-flick.mp3` (or `.wav`)
   - The opening launch audio: "Welcome to Flick".
   - Plays immediately on app launch and finishes sounding before transiting into auth/home.

2. `chat-notification.mp3`
   - Played when receiving a direct message / chat ("{Caller} just sent you a chat").

3. `like-notification.mp3`
   - Played when someone likes a flick.

4. `comment-notification.mp3`
   - Played when someone comments on a flick.

5. `story-notification.mp3`
   - Played when a new story is posted.

6. `notification.mp3`
   - Default general notification chime.
